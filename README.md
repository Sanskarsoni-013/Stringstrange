## 🌟 Game Highlights & Innovations

- **Chrono Time Dilation (Bullet-Time)**:
  - **Press & Hold / Tap**: Fire impulse afterburners at full 1.0x velocity.
  - **Release**: Time bends smoothly down to **0.45x tactical slow-motion**, granting the pilot precision to calculate angles through tight laser pylons.
- **Overclock Hyperdrive (100% Reactor)**:
  - Grazing laser beams (*Close Calls*) and collecting hexagonal energy rings charges your reactor core.
  - At 100%, **OVERCLOCK HYPERDRIVE** activates for 4.5 seconds: time accelerates to 2.2x speed, your interceptor gains invulnerability, and blasts directly through laser gates, shattering them into hundreds of explosive polygon shards!
- **Procedural Web Audio Synthesizer**:
  - Zero external sound files — 100% synthesized in the browser with the Web Audio API.
  - Clean upward flap impulses, harmonic arpeggio chimes on ring collection, electric laser chirps, and cinematic explosion rumbles.
- **Bright & Illuminated Aero Themes**:
  - ☀️ **Aero Lumina**: Bright celestial blue with daylight sunshine.
  - 🌆 **Sunset Horizon**: Warm magenta & radiant sunset gold.
  - 🌙 **Cyber Aurora**: Luminous emerald & mint glow.
- **Universal Responsive Controls**:
  - **Desktop**: Click anywhere, or press `Spacebar`, `Up Arrow`, or `W`. Press `[P]` to pause.
  - **Mobile & Tablet**: Tap anywhere with one finger (pinch-to-zoom and gesture interference fully disabled).

```bash

npm start

# Build production bundle
npm run build
```
